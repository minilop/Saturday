Analyze and trace the business context around a specific point in an existing
  codebase. Use when the user wants to understand which business processes
  use, depend on, or are affected by a specific database table, field, entity,
  class, method, API, event, Job, external table, data source, or configuration.
  Start from the user's specified point and expand outward through code
  references, call relationships, data flows, dependencies, and related
  business operations to identify and explain the connected business logic.
